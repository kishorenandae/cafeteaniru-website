More Than Just Tea

Cafe Teaniru is a cozy bamboo-hut inspired cafe crafted around the love for great tea, simple food and meaningful conversations.

We bring people together over freshly prepared tea, delicious snacks and a relaxed bamboo-hut ambience. Whether it’s a quick tea break or a long catch-up with friends, we want every visit to feel special and authentic.
