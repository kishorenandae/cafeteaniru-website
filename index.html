About Us

Built for chai lovers and coffee moments

Café Teaniru brings together authentic flavors, cozy interiors, and a welcoming community vibe inspired by traditional Indian tea houses and modern café culture.

Since 2026 Fresh tea, coffee & comfort

Founded with love by Kishore, Café Teaniru was created to be more than a café—it’s a place for conversations, quiet work sessions, family hangouts, and memorable evenings over hot chai and handcrafted coffee.

From earthy kulhad chai to rich filter coffee and comforting snacks, every item reflects warmth, hospitality, and quality.
